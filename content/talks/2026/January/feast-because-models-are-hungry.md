## Abstract

Machine learning models are only as good as the data behind them. While Kubeflow excels at pipelines, training, and serving, managing features between raw data and models remains a common challenge.

This lightning talk introduces Feast, an open-source feature store that bridges raw data and Kubeflow workflows. We’ll cover what Feast is, why it’s used, and how it helps teams deliver consistent, production-ready features for both training and serving.

We’ll also discuss how contributing to Feast strengthens the broader Kubeflow and MLOps ecosystem, and share easy ways to get started with Feast contributions. Whether you’re new to MLOps or looking to contribute to open source, this talk highlights why better data leads to better models.
